## Frozen trial

`forward:step11-20260907` registers once on September 7, 2026. Its four participants
are the three Individual portfolios and an Index-led council portfolio, with a
separate matched VTI sleeve and an explicit zero-interest cash baseline. Every
portfolio begins with USD10,000 fictional cash, FIFO accounting and exact TWR.
There are no later contributions, withdrawals, dividend reinvestment or terminal
liquidation. Risk limits, opportunity coverage, costs, versions and stopping rules
are frozen before decisions.

The runtime is **deterministic foundation-informed rules, not new LLM deliberation
or a validated trading system**. Index buys an initial bounded broad-fund sleeve;
Value abstains because bars do not establish valuation or a margin of safety;
Trend buys only if the last completed close exceeds the preceding 20 session
highs. The council reviews the three opinions but retains its fixed Index lead
allocation. No causal advantage from advice is claimed. No intrawindow tuning,
new recommendations or model spend is authorized by this trial.

The query covers completed sessions August 6–September 4. All participants see
the same sealed complete VTI snapshot or all abstain. Data has a ten-minute
deadline; opinions and sized orders have a fifteen-minute deadline. Crashed
reserved rounds abstain instead of repeating model work. An interrupted order
commit may resume only before its original deadline.

Whole shares are sized as 25% of initial cash divided by a cap of 110% of the
frozen historical close. The matched market sleeve uses the same rule, rather
than silently comparing a fully invested benchmark to a small strategy sleeve.
Simulated execution uses the first future daily close, with 5 basis points of
slippage plus 1 basis point of half-spread and zero commission. A price above the
cap leaves the order unfilled. Orders expire immediately after that close. The
same model applies to every portfolio. No order is sent to a broker.

Future sessions are September 8, 9, 10, 11 and 14. One explicitly uncalibrated
0.5 control forecast asks whether the fifth close exceeds the frozen historical
close; an intervening split makes it unscorable. This is a small process trial,
well below the 60-session/30-forecast paper-review evidence floors.

Observation is manual, once the window has elapsed. Its fixed review begins
**September 15 at 00:20:01 UTC (September 14 at 7:20:01 p.m. Chicago time)**.
Two fixed outcome queries have four attempts each. Initial decision retrieval
has four attempts, so the entire trial has at most twelve account attempts,
separate from the earlier two qualification requests. All jobs use the same
existing account quota. Stop is **September 15 at 22:00 UTC**; an unobserved
window stops incomplete, without shifting dates or backfilling decisions.

Outcome retrieval applies the previously committed orders mechanically to daily
closes. Ledger effective times are the scheduled economic closes; creation and
receipt times remain the actual later observation. These are bar-based research
fills, not evidence of contemporaneously available live execution. Raw marks,
dividend entitlements/payments and supported splits pass through the v2 reducer.
Ambiguous action ordering, pending-order split reconciliation and fractional
cash-in-lieu remain visible gaps, not invented settlements.

Recurring incremental model/data costs are USD0. Existing local hardware and
electricity are unknown, so complete economics net remains null. One-time
learning and development costs are separately unknown. Trading costs are already
included in the simulated fills; the ETF expense ratio is not charged twice.
