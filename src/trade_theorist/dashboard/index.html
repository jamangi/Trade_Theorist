How to read these results

Net return includes fees and distributions once. Drawdown is loss from the highest recorded equity. Exposure is holdings value divided by equity. Turnover is absolute traded notional divided by mean daily equity. Volatility is daily, not annualized. Brier score measures binary-forecast error; lower is better, and only matured, scorable forecasts count.

Blank results have explicit reasons. Missing sessions are not bridged by invented returns. The cash baseline earns zero interest; the separately funded index baseline uses the same trading costs and dividend convention. Hindsight, fixture and forward trials are never ranked together.
