How to read these results

Equity is cash plus eligible marked holdings and dividend receivables. FIFO acquisition fees enter basis; disposal fees reduce realized gain. TWR links returns at each external flow, so deposits and withdrawals are not investment gains or losses. Drawdown follows that flow-neutral wealth.

Charts show each portfolio's own dollars; deposits can change the equity line without changing TWR. Exact history is available in tables. Baselines require matching flow timing and costs. Trading TWR includes execution costs; recurring operating costs are shown separately. Missing values have reasons and are never treated as zero.

Turnover is gross traded notional divided by mean scheduled equity. Volatility is unannualized sample deviation. Closed-position hit rate and FIFO relief win rate use different samples. Brier measures matured forecast error; a lower value is better. Simulated and broker-paper results are separate observations, never a ranking of reasoning skill.

About this saved view

Filters, evidence panels and historical selection use the loaded report only. They cannot run models, contact an account or place an order. A new report is prepared through the local workflow. Missing learning, forecast or heartbeat records remain explicitly unknown.
